# Books & Media That Shaped My Thinking

## Interstellar (film)
The preciousness of time. The heavy consequences of certain choices — especially around spending time with your kids. Time is the one resource you can't get back, and some doors close permanently.

## The Three-Body Problem (Liu Cixin)
Technology shapes society. Beliefs shape society. Technology shapes beliefs. These feedback loops play out over centuries and civilizations. Thinking very long-term appeals to me — zooming out far enough to see the real forces at work.

## Hell Yes or No (Derek Sivers)
Guide your goals and decisions by enthusiasm. If something doesn't spark a strong yes, treat it as a no. A framework for protecting focus and energy in a world with too many options.
