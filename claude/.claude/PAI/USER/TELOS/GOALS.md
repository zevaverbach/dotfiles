# Goals

## Short-Term (Next 3 Months — by June 2026)

- **Graduate from mid-level to senior engineer** — Close gaps in CS fundamentals, system architecture, and network topology. The goal isn't academic knowledge — it's working more effectively with agents by understanding *why* things work, not just *that* they work. Learn through every coding session, not just dedicated study time.
- **Master agentic engineering workflows** — Be able to orchestrate swarms of agents to build quality software while staying hands-on with critical architecture and code review. Not just delegating blindly — actually knowing what I'm doing in both the building and the reviewing.
- **Ship meaningful progress on Noko** — The family manager app serves double duty: a real product I care about AND a test bed for picking up agentic engineering skills. Native/Electron + mobile.
- **Learn from the best practitioners** — Steve Yegge's Vibe Coding work, O'Reilly's AI Engineering material, IndyDevDan's course. Go deep into these resources.

## Medium-Term (1-2 Years)

- **Portfolio of income streams** — Build toward multiple revenue-generating bets: products, consulting, investments. Not dependent on any single salary or client.
- **Proficiency in native/Electron and mobile development** — Expand from web generalist into shipping desktop and mobile apps confidently, with AI-assisted workflows as a force multiplier.
- **Establish a repeatable build-and-ship muscle** — Get fast at going from idea to shipped product using agent orchestration.

## Long-Term (5+ Years)

- **Financial freedom** — Passive/recurring income exceeds expenses. Full choice over how I spend my time.
- **Time with my kids** — The preciousness of time is real. Financial freedom is partly in service of being present for the people who matter most.
- **Ownership over employment** — Equity in things I've built, not trading hours for a salary.
