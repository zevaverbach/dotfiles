# Influential Thinkers & People

## Steve Yegge
Very experienced engineer who is diving headfirst into Agentic Engineering. Has my attention because he's walking the path I want to walk — deep technical credibility combined with willingness to fully embrace the AI shift. His Vibe Coding work is a key reference.

## Kurt Vonnegut
The sheer humanity, warmth, and absurdity of life. A reminder that the point of all this building and striving is the human experience underneath it.

## Stephen Malkmus
It's okay to be detached. Sometimes good to cocoon and be. Not everything needs to be optimized or performed. A counterweight to hustle culture.

## Al Averbach (my dad)
Told me to take it easy. Helped me become a critical thinker and somewhat skeptical. The combination of calmness and intellectual rigor — don't rush, but don't accept things uncritically either.

## Jeff Erkelens (and his dad Bill Erkelens)
Hard workers and hard players who built wealth with their own two hands. Have no issue fully ignoring trends. A model for building real things through effort and independence, not chasing what's fashionable.
