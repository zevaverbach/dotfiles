# Challenges

## Current Obstacles

- **Time and focus** — Too many things competing for attention. Hard to go deep when pulled in multiple directions. The tension between breadth (generalist instincts, multiple bets) and depth (mastery requires sustained focus).
- **Skills gap in agentic engineering** — Transitioning into AI/LLM-powered development. The field is moving fast — new patterns, tools, and best practices are emerging weekly. Need to get proficient enough to orchestrate agents effectively, not just use them superficially.
- **Skills gap in native/mobile** — Generalist web background, now pushing into Electron and mobile app development. New territory with different constraints.
- **Balancing learning with shipping** — Noko is both a real product and a learning vehicle. The tension: move fast enough to ship, slow down enough to actually learn the underlying patterns.
- **Going from intuitive to explicit** — Self-described intuitive builder who wants more explicit frameworks. The challenge is formalizing decision-making without losing the speed and instinct that got me here.
