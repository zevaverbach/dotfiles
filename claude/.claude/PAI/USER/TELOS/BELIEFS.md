# Beliefs

## Core Beliefs

- **Craft matters.** Quality and depth of skill is the ultimate competitive advantage. Do the thing well.
- **Leverage > hours.** Working smarter beats working harder — tools, automation, compounding. The right systems let one person do the work of ten.
- **Ownership > employment.** Building equity in your own things beats trading time for a salary. The asymmetry is too large to ignore.
- **Being kind is a differentiator.** Especially in the agentic age, where technical skills commoditize, soft skills and genuine warmth become increasingly rare and valuable.
- **Technology shapes society; beliefs shape society; technology shapes beliefs.** These feedback loops are real and play out over long timescales. Think long-term about the forces shaping the world.
- **Hell Yes or No.** Guide decisions by enthusiasm. If it's not a strong yes, it's a no. Protects focus and energy.
- **Time is precious.** Some choices carry heavy consequences — especially choices about where you spend your time and who you spend it with.
