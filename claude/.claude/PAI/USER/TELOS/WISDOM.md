# Wisdom

Hard-won lessons and insights collected over time.

## Exercise creates energy.
Counterintuitive but true. Spending energy on physical movement generates more energy than it costs. Not just physical — mental clarity, emotional resilience, creativity all improve. The investment always pays off.

## Quitting because it's hard isn't a good idea.
Hard is not the same as wrong. The difficulty is often the point — it's where the growth and differentiation happen. Quit because it's wrong, not because it's hard.

## Go all the way into the thing you're obsessed with.
Seek out resources even if they're not offered in your environment. Don't wait for permission or a curriculum. If something has your attention, chase it fully — find the people, the books, the courses, the communities. The environment won't hand it to you.

## Finishing things feels good.
The satisfaction of completion is real and underrated. Shipping beats perfecting. A finished thing — even a rough one — teaches more and feels better than an elegant thing that never lands.
